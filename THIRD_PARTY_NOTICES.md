# Third-party notices

S-Clip depends on open-source projects listed in
[`pyproject.toml`](./pyproject.toml). Their packages retain their respective
copyright and licence notices.

The interface bundles two typefaces from the
[Google Fonts](https://github.com/google/fonts) collection:

- **Figtree**, licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The full licence
  is included at
  [`src/sclip/ui/assets/fonts/OFL-Figtree.txt`](./src/sclip/ui/assets/fonts/OFL-Figtree.txt).
- **Unbounded**, licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The full licence
  is included at
  [`src/sclip/ui/assets/fonts/OFL-Unbounded.txt`](./src/sclip/ui/assets/fonts/OFL-Unbounded.txt).

FFmpeg is an external runtime dependency and is not distributed in the source
repository. Its licence depends on the build and enabled codecs. Refer to the
[FFmpeg legal page](https://ffmpeg.org/legal.html) for details.
